# Sources and assets

Wildwise business code is independently written and licensed under MIT. The
license does not extend to Klei's game, game assets, or other authors' mods.

The installable archive contains Lua source and project documentation. It
references the fonts, widgets, map APIs, inventory images, minisign prefab and
placement outlines supplied by the user's installed Don't Starve Together.
It does not redistribute those assets or require a reference mod at runtime.

Reference source was read to understand behavior, integration points and
failure modes. No reference mod's source, icon, font or texture is included.
In particular, Insight's RECEX license is not treated as permission to copy
its implementation into this MIT project.

The original references and exact research snapshots are documented in
[the research record](https://github.com/Morilence/Wildwise/blob/main/docs/research.md).
Commitlint and its npm dependencies are development tools only; their licenses
remain in their respective packages and they are excluded from the mod archive.
